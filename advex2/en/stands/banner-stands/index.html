01Can a banner stand be used outdoors?+

Standard lightweight models are intended for indoor use. Outdoor stability and fixings must be specified separately.

02What determines the cost of Banner stands?+

The selected format, size, material, quantity and graphics. Add your specifications to the form; pricing is confirmed in the estimate.
