FAQ

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Have another question? Tell us what you have in mind in your request.

01How much does an exhibition stand cost?+

Cost depends on area, structure, materials and equipment. This prototype has no confirmed rates; use the estimate form.

02What is the production lead time?+

The schedule depends on complexity and exhibition dates. Include your event date in the request.

03Can I request a bespoke design?+

The inquiry flow supports bespoke projects. Attach brand guidelines, a venue plan or references.

04Are installation and dismantling included?+

The agreed estimate defines the scope. Installation and dismantling depend on venue requirements.

05Can I rent equipment separately?+

Build a separate inquiry for furniture, cases and multimedia in the catalog. Availability and terms require confirmation.

06When should I get in touch?+

When choosing your exhibition space. Knowing dimensions and regulations early makes it easier to develop options.
